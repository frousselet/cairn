Rendered inside the obligation's detail page and swapped into `#notification-filings` by `incidents:notification-filings` after a filing, so it must stand on its own. Context : `notification`, `filings` (ordered by `submitted_at` ascending - the narrative order, oldest first, which is how a phased provision reads) and `filing_outcomes`, the choices the recipient's answer may take. **Append-only, and the markup says so.** No edit control and no delete control : what the organisation told a regulator is never rewritten, and a correction is a further filing that names the one it replaces. Exactly three fields may be completed after the insert, once each, and the only control offered here is the one that records the recipient's answer through that narrow route. `is_correction` and `supersedes` are made visible on the row rather than folded away : a supplementary filing that adds information and a corrective filing that retracts an earlier one are different acts, and reading the log without seeing which is which is how a phased provision is mistaken for a contradiction. {% endcomment %} {% has_perm "incidents.notification.update" as can_update_notification %}
