Redesign mockups

    What needs action first, then one card per question : compliance, risks (one matrix at a time), indicators, objectives furthest from their target.

  • 02Risks

    Lifecycle steps as filters, a list that keeps what decides a click, a preview beside it on wide screens, cards on a phone.

  • 03Risk page

    RISK-1 : an On this page column on wide screens, related records folded with their counts, the next step in a bar at the bottom of a phone.

  • 04New essential asset

    Create short in a side sheet that keeps the list in view, full screen on a phone.

  • 05Ask Cairn

    The answer and its records first, how it was built folded; the palette takes the whole screen of a phone.
