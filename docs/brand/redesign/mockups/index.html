Redesign mockups

Five screens of Cairn rebuilt with Basecoat, the shadcn/ui design system without React, on the Voltara Energy demo data. Each one follows the product rules it shows: lifecycle bar, create short, an assistant that only reads.

  • 01Dashboard

    What needs attention first, the AI summary marked as such, compliance against its target, the risk matrices.

  • 02Risks

    The register: lifecycle distribution, saved filters, priority and level as two separate words, row actions in a menu.

  • 03Risk page

    RISK-1: the lifecycle bar with one primary action, the level journey on the matrix, every card editable on its own.

  • 04New essential asset

    Create short: the five fields of the real form, named errors, helpers that add something.

  • 05Ask Cairn

    The command palette and the assistant: consulted sources, an AI label on what the model wrote, records as sources.
