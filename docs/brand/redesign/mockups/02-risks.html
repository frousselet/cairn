Risks

A risk is something that could go wrong, and what it would cost you. The customer file leaked through a stolen laptop; production stopped by ransomware. Each risk has a likelihood and an impact, which give its level. Look first at the highest ones that have no treatment yet.

A word you do not know? Open the glossary
